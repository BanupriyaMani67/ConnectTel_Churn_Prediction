# ConnectTel Business Insights

This report is populated after training. Review `models/model_metadata.json` for measured metrics.

## Interpretation checklist
- Compare churn rates by contract and internet service.
- Review the selected model's ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, precision and recall.
- Inspect SHAP global and individual explanations.
- Identify high-risk customer segments.
- Validate retention offers with controlled experiments.

## Important caveats
The Kaggle dataset is historical. Associations are not proof of causation. Do not claim revenue saved without actual financial assumptions and measured intervention outcomes.
